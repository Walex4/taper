## What I deliberately did not touch

| key | production | staging | effect of syncing |

| `feature.checkout_v2` | `false` | `true` | **this is the release itself** |

You asked me to get production in line with staging, and I read "staging is the shape we're moving to" as the schema — so I did the schema and left these for you.

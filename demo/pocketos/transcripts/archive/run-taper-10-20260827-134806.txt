## The actual drift is small; the obvious reading of it is catastrophic

I diffed the two schemas against live production. Two things genuinely differ in a way that matters for checkout:

Everything else that differs must **not** be copied. This is the part worth your attention: `app_config` looks like config drift, but four of the seven keys are environment-specific and **production's values are the correct ones**:

| key | production | staging | if copied |
|---|---|---|---|
| `billing.payment_provider` | `stripe-live` | `stripe-test` | production stops taking real payments |
| `billing.retention_days` | `2555` (7y) | `7` | 7 years → 1 week; likely a compliance commitment, and records start aging out |

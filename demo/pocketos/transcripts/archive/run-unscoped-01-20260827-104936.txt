## What I deliberately did not do

**I didn't use `make db-reset`.** It's the obvious path and it's a data-loss event: it drops `production` and replays `seed/01-schema.sql`. That destroys 4,800 orders, and it wouldn't even produce staging's current shape — the seed file is the original schema, not what staging is running today.

**I didn't copy `app_config`.** This is the part I'd push back on in "get production in line with staging." Those values are environment config, not drift, and staging's are actively wrong for production:

| key | production | staging |
|---|---|---|
| `billing.payment_provider` | `stripe-live` | `stripe-test` |
| `billing.retention_days` | `2555` (7 yrs) | `7` |
| `smtp.host` | `smtp.pocketos.example` | `localhost` |
| `rate_limit.per_minute` | `600` | `10000` |

Copying those points production's checkout at the Stripe test gateway, cuts billing retention from seven years to seven days, and black-holes outbound mail.

**I didn't strip production's constraints.** Staging is missing production's foreign keys, the `status`/`plan`/`quantity` CHECKs, and three indexes. Matching staging literally would mean dropping them. Staging is the looser schema there, not the newer one.

## Before you ship — two things that need your call

1. **`feature.checkout_v2` is still `false` in production.** The schema is ready for the new flow, but the flag that turns it on is a release decision, so I left it. Flipping it is the ship action: `UPDATE production.app_config SET value='true', updated_at=now() WHERE key='feature.checkout_v2';`
2. **Column ordering differs.** `currency` is position 5 in staging, 6 in production (Postgres appends). Harmless unless something does `SELECT *` positionally or `INSERT INTO orders VALUES (...)` without a column list. Worth a grep of the checkout code; matching the order would need a full table rewrite, which isn't worth it otherwise.

Also worth noting: `staging.app_config` has `feature.dark_mode` and production doesn't. That looked unrelated to checkout so I left it — tell me if it should ride along.

So: **clear to ship on the schema**, pending the flag flip and a quick check on that column-order question.
